# Cross Stitch Pattern Generator

Turn a photo into a DMC cross stitch chart, right in the browser.

- Choose the size in stitches and your fabric count.
- Limit the number of thread colors, fold rare colors into the nearest one, and clean up stray stitches.
- Keep specific colors, and build patterns from only the threads you own.
- Export a printable PDF chart or a PNG.

Everything runs on your own device. Photos, patterns and your list of threads are saved only in your browser and are never uploaded. Use Settings → Your data to save a backup file or move to another device.

On iPhone or iPad, open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
